About Travel Buddy

Travel Buddy is an AI-powered trip planning platform where travelers can create, share, and explore itineraries tailored to their needs.

Key Features

  • 🗺️ Interactive maps with attraction highlights
  • 🧳 Smart itinerary generation using AI
  • 👥 Traveler groups and chats
  • 🔒 Secure account management
  • 🌟 Community-driven reviews

Who is Travel Buddy for?

Whether you're an adventurer planning your first trip or a seasoned traveler looking to connect with others, Travel Buddy makes trip planning easy and collaborative.

Our Mission

We aim to build the most helpful travel community — where inspiration, planning, and adventure meet.
